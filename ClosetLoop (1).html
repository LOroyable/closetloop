About ClosetLoop

A fashion-only marketplace that connects people who want to sell with people who want to save.

The problem we solve

Many people own clothes and accessories they rarely wear, and these items end up stored in closets or thrown away. ClosetLoop turns them into income for sellers and gives buyers good-quality pre-loved pieces at reasonable prices, all in one place.

Our goals

  • Affordable fashion for customers
  • Income from items you no longer use
  • Simple, convenient buying and selling
  • Less clothing waste through reuse

Our vision

To make ClosetLoop a trusted and well-known platform for buying and selling fashion in Saudi Arabia, with more users, a wider selection and an easier experience.

How ClosetLoop earns

ClosetLoop is a consumer-to-consumer (C2C) marketplace. We earn a 5% commission on each successful sale: an item sold for SAR 100 earns ClosetLoop SAR 5. In the future, sellers may also pay for Paid Product Promotions to make their items more visible.

Help and support

We reply within 24 hours.

How can I list an item for sale?

Create a seller account, open List an item, add photos, size, condition and price, then publish.

How can I track my order?

Open Account, then My orders, to follow each step from order placed to delivered.

What payment methods are available?

Mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay and Visa or Mastercard cards, all on a secure payment page.

How do I know an item matches its description?

Sellers list condition, flaws and photos. If it does not match, you can return it within 3 days of delivery.

When does the seller get paid?

After you confirm delivery. Until then the payment stays with ClosetLoop.

How do I return an item?

Open My orders, choose Request return and follow the steps. Refunds go to your original payment method.
